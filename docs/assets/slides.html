01
The problem

Everyone lets the model size the bet.
That's the part it is worst at.

In Nof1's Alpha Arena, four of six language models lost money trading live. GPT-5 finished down 63%, Gemini down 57%, Grok down 58%. The winner's edge was sizing discipline, not prediction.

Independent replication of LLM trading research finds the edge disappears out of sample. A 2026 audit rated zero of nineteen such studies at the top reproducibility tier.

Models are good at
reading
Regime narration, news, choosing between framed options.
Models are bad at
arithmetic
Position sizing, strike selection, respecting a limit under pressure.
02
The inversion

The model returns an index.

Deterministic code builds every candidate structure from the live chain, sizes it, and refuses anything failing a gate. Only then is the model asked to choose. 

{"action": "trade", "refs": ["QQQ:call_debit_spread"], "confidence": 0.8}

That is its entire output space. It cannot invent a structure, move a strike, change a quantity, or widen a limit, because none of those are things it can say.

The worst a confused or prompt-injected model can do is pick a slightly worse safe trade, or refuse to trade.

03
Architecture

Four roles. One of them is allowed to be wrong.

Scout
measures trend, realized vol, implied vol, and the spread between them
↓
Analyst
Gemini + Google Search. What is actually happening today
↓
Strategist
Gemini + 7 tools. Investigates, compares structures, decides
↓
Risk officer
deterministic. Sizes it or refuses it. No model call in the file
↓
Executor
Alpaca CLI. Every order is a reproducible shell command
↓
Manager
profit target, stop, and a flatten before the assignment window
04
Risk gates

Seven gates. Numbered, tested, journalled by name.

GateLimit
G1Kill switches−3% on the day flattens · −8% drawdown ends the event
G2Trade budget8 new structures a day, 10 open at once
G3Defined riskEvery short leg needs same-expiry cover. No flag disables it
G4Leg liquidityBid/ask within 12% of mid, per leg
G5PricingSell no spread under 18% of width · pay no more than 45%
G6Event blackoutNo writing premium across a scheduled catalyst
G7Sizing0.75% of equity per structure · 6% total · 3% convex

Priced at the touch, not the mid. Alpaca's paper engine fills against the NBBO, so sizing off mid prices understates real maximum loss.

05
The differentiator

We show you the trades it refused.

risk_officer  SPY  rejected   G5 pricing: credit 17.0% of width
                             is below the 18% floor
risk_officer  IWM  rejected   G5 pricing: credit 13.0% of width
risk_officer  QQQ  approved   G3 all short legs covered by
                             same-expiry long legs

Every refusal is attributed to the gate that caused it and published on the live dashboard beside the trades that passed.

An agent that shows only its trades is showing half its behaviour — and the half it refused is the half that explains the risk.

06
Alpaca infrastructure

Both interfaces, split on purpose.

CLI — execution
reproducible
Every order is alpaca api POST /v2/orders with a JSON body, stored in the journal beside the proposal and the risk verdict that produced it. A judge can paste any trade into a terminal.
MCP — research
read-only
41 tools over one stdio session for news and market state, with output wrapped in a trust envelope marking it as data rather than instructions.

Execution deliberately avoids MCP: its v2 multi-leg order bug (issue #97) sends the legs array as a raw string, and every structure this agent trades is multi-leg.

Verified end to end: a spread opened at a net 1.05 debit and closed as a package for a net 1.05 credit.

08
Honest limits

What we would not claim.

Paper fills are more generous than live ones, especially on wide quotes, so any result here is optimistic by construction. The free data feed delays trades by fifteen minutes.

No historical option prices are available on this plan, so the replay harness prices entry and exit with the same model and simulates no volatility risk. It validates mechanics, not edge — and we say so in the tool's own output.

Four and a half sessions is far too short a sample to distinguish skill from luck. We would say that whichever way the number lands on Friday.
